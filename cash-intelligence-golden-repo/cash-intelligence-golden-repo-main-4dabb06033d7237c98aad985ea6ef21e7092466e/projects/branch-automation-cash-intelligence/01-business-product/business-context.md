# Business Context

**Source-backed.** `SRC-CONF-CIR-001` states the following and nothing more about the business context:

**Objective.** Define the requirements for the Cash Intelligence Dashboard, an at-a-glance landing page that summarises cash operations across all views and links to each detailed page.

**Background.** The Cash Intelligence prototype (cash-analytics) presents a Dashboard composed of seven clickable summary widgets. Each widget summarises a deeper page and is driven by configurable thresholds defined in Settings. The page documents the Dashboard requirements and the default threshold settings that drive widget calculations.

**Not stated in source:** business problem statement, target customers or institutions, commercial drivers, success measures, timeline. These remain open (see `02-requirements/assumptions-dependencies-open-questions.md`).

The user-story wording indicates the intended users are cash operations users, service coordinators, analysts, admins and contracted service providers at a financial institution operating ATMs and TCR/TCD devices across regions and branches/sites (see `personas-and-roles.md`).
